Every image the game uses or was made from: gfx/ (the sprite and tile sheets and palettes that gfxtool.js builds GfxData.cls from; *_x4 are 4x copies to look at), tools/ previews (people_preview.png: every face, parts_preview.png: every sprite part) and pictures, and the screenshots.
The art is generated by tools/art_*.py (and scenes.py, people_preview.py where present): run those to rebuild.
test/ (if present): the bots and screenshot scripts used to play-test it in the emulator (TOOLS_assets.zip has nextemu.py; paths inside point at /home/claude/work/...).
