POLY

A FILLED-POLYGON 3D FLIGHT DEMO FOR THE ZX SPECTRUM NEXT!

Solid faces. Full hidden-surface removal. Dithered colours. All in 256 colours on Layer 2, at 28MHz.

POLY is a 3D engine demo in the spirit of 3D Starstrike II on the Amstrad CPC, pushed as far as the Next will go.

THE FLIGHT

You start in an asteroid field with a planet in the distance. The autopilot takes you:

- Through the asteroids
- Down towards the planet, through its sky
- Over a city of about 600 buildings
- Across the rooftops, then up and out again

WHAT'S UNDER THE HOOD

- Solid filled polygons with hidden-surface removal and checker-dithered shading, from 16 colour ramps with 16 shades each
- A planet with an atmosphere rim and sunlit bands from space, which turns into a horizon with ground below and sky above as you get close
- A procedural city of 48 x 48 cells with roads and 8 kinds of building, drawn with less detail the farther away they are
- Level of detail on every object, from full models down to low-poly shapes, a hexagon and a dot
- Double-buffered Layer 2 graphics, with the engine written in hand-tuned Z80 and the demo in NTC BASIC

This is a technical demo that pushes the Next hard, so the frame rate varies with how much is on screen.

CONTROLS

None. It's a demo, and the autopilot flies the whole loop. Sit back and watch.

Requires a ZX Spectrum Next (28MHz).

Fly to the planet. Land on the city. Watch the polygons go.
