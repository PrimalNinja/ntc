PI01 LINK - the card and the Pi
-------------------------------
Status byte at &FD81: &FF  (bit 0 a byte waiting, bit 1 room to write)
Card: CPC-CPLink (or none: bits 4-7 are not 1010)
&FF: nothing answers at &FD81 - no card, or not a CPLink at this address.
  FAIL  a card at &FD81  []

0 ok, 1 failed

--- ended  bytes to the Pi 0, from the Pi 0, Pi ROM writes 0  (none)
