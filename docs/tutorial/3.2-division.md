# 3.2 Division, Mod and powers

*3 Calculations and maths*

`/` and `\` both divide and give a whole number, cut toward 0. `Mod` is the remainder.

```basic
Print 7 / 2; ","; -7 \ 2; ","; 17 Mod 5; ","; -7 Mod 2
Print 2 ^ 10; ","; 3 ^ 4
```

```text
3,-3,2,-1
1024,81
```

- `Mod` has the sign of the left side.
- `^` is a whole number power; a negative power gives 0.
- In VB, `7 / 2` is 3.5. NTC has no fractions, so `/` is the same as `\`.

To keep part of a fraction, scale up first: tenths of a value `a / b` are `a * 10 \ b`.

```basic
Dim total As Integer, n As Integer
total = 17 : n = 4
Print "average "; total \ n; "."; (total * 10 \ n) Mod 10
```

```text
average 4.2
```
