Every image the game uses or was made from: gfx/ (the sprite and tile sheets and palettes that gfxtool.js builds GfxData.cls from; *_x4 are 4x copies to look at), tools/ previews and pictures, and the screenshots.
The art is generated by tools/art_*.py (and scenes.py where there is one): run those to rebuild gfx/.
test/ (if present): the bots and screenshot scripts used to play-test it in the emulator (TOOLS_assets.zip has nextemu.py; paths inside point at /home/claude/work/...).
