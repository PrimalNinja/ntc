OSM Cut

Cuts an OpenStreetMap .osm.pbf down to what STATE3D needs: buildings (heights, levels, names), roads, railways, coastline, water, woods and parks, trees, peaks, towers and bridges, inside the box. Everything runs in this page; nothing is sent anywhere.

1. The .osm.pbf file
2. The box (degrees)

Default: Auckland from the CBD and Ponsonby north to Albany, out to Rangitoto, Devonport and Birkdale.
